This is what you get

Every plan is drawn on your real street. Signs sit at the right spacing for the speed, flagger stations are placed and numbered, and side streets get their own signing where the manual calls for it.

Samples are drawn from real map data and show the style of what we deliver. Your plan is built from your own site and answers.

What is in every plan

A plan your inspector can read at a glance and your crew can set up from.

Site-specific plan sheet

Your street, your lanes, your speed. Signs, tapers, buffers and devices with spacing from the CA MUTCD and Caltrans T-series plans.

Sign and device schedule

Every sign by code and distance, and the cone and device counts, so the crew loads the truck once.

Pedestrians and detours

Sidewalk closures, walkways, crossing routes and full-closure detours, with the accessible route spelled out.

Permit-ready notes

Title block, plan notes and submittal items filled in for the agency reviewing it, so the package is not bounced for missing details.

Field checklist

Setup order and a pre-work walk checklist for your foreman, so what is on the street matches what was approved.

Revisions included

If the agency comes back with comments on the plan we prepared, we revise it.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Who prepares the plans?

A certified Traffic Control Supervisor with field experience on California streets. Each plan is checked against the CA MUTCD and Caltrans Standard Plans before it goes out.

Will the city accept it?

We build the plan to the standards cities use when reviewing traffic control, and fill in the notes and submittal items for your agency. Agencies can still ask for changes, and we revise what we prepared. Work on state highways may need a Caltrans encroachment permit and, in some cases, a plan stamped by an engineer. Tell us in the form and we will say what applies.

What if I do not know every answer?

Choose "Not sure." We confirm the open items with you before drawing the plan, and we check the street itself from map data.

How does payment work?

You answer the plan questions first and choose a plan. We reply with the total and payment instructions, and we start once payment is received. Complex jobs are quoted first.

Do you set up the traffic control?

This service is for the plan and paperwork. If you also need crews, devices or flaggers, mention it in the notes and we will follow up.

Is the plan a substitute for checking the site?

No. A plan is drawn from road data and your answers. The crew walks the site, compares it with the plan and reports any difference before setting up.
